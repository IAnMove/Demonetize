# Proposal: push-neon-signal-choir-payoff

## Why
Signal Choir exists as a rescue-themed elite, but it should have a stronger in-run identity once equipped.

## What Changes
- Add player-side rescue reward modifiers.
- Let Signal Choir increase rescue score and orb burst rewards.
- Give rescue pickups a distinct visual treatment while Signal Choir is active.

## Success Criteria
- Signal Choir makes rescue saves feel visibly and mechanically richer.
- The change reuses existing rescue, pickup, and render systems.
