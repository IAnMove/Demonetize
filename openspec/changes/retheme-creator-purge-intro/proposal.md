# Proposal: retheme-creator-purge-intro

## Why
The current neon sci-fi frame is functional, but generic.
A creator-platform apocalypse gives the game a sharper hook: authentic creators are being erased by an automated purge that was built to fight mass-produced fake content, but now cannot reliably tell humans from machines.

This direction can make the game easier to pitch, easier to understand, and more culturally charged without changing the proven arena-survival loop.

## What Changes
- Retheme the fiction from a generic Overseer siege into a creator-authenticity purge inside a parody video platform
- Add a short cinematic intro before the title screen with one editable epic paragraph
- Rename major lore surfaces: boss, enemies, pickups, rescues, upgrades, wave labels, game-over, and victory copy
- Keep all core combat mechanics intact during the first pass
- Make the intro skippable and readable on desktop and mobile
- Keep all narrative copy centralized so it can be tuned quickly

## Success Criteria
- A new player understands the fantasy in under 10 seconds: survive the purge and save real creators
- The intro feels cinematic and urgent without delaying restart or first-play flow
- The retheme strengthens the existing mechanics instead of requiring a rebuild
- The copy avoids direct real-company accusations by using parody platform language
- The title screen, HUD, upgrade cards, boss labels, and post-run screens all feel like one coherent world

## Non-Goals
- No legal or journalistic claim about a real platform
- No external news feed or real-world data dependency
- No new gameplay systems in this change
- No accounts, uploads, online services, or creator identity features
- No long unskippable cutscene
