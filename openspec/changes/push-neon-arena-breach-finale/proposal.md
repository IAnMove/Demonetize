# Proposal: push-neon-arena-breach-finale

## Why
The boss collapse now lands better, but the arena itself still stays too stable during the finale.
A short breach effect in the floor will make the ending feel more catastrophic without turning into a full cutscene.

## What Changes
- Add a short arena-breach visual effect triggered by the final Overseer collapse
- Expand the collapse read with floor cracks, a breach core, and stronger ground response
- Keep the effect render-only and tied to the existing `boss-collapse-start` event

## Success Criteria
- The arena visibly destabilizes during the final collapse
- The breach effect is readable but short
- The implementation stays lightweight and does not change gameplay rules
