# Proposal: push-neon-rescue-cache-draft

## Why
Rescue performance should influence build decisions, not only score. Unlocking an extra elite choice at chapter transition makes the rescue layer strategically relevant.

## What Changes
- Add a rescue threshold before chapter transition.
- Unlock one extra elite upgrade choice when the threshold is met.
- Surface that rescue cache bonus in the chapter transition UI.

## Success Criteria
- Rescue play changes the quality of chapter transition drafting.
- The chapter reward screen clearly explains why the extra choice appeared.
