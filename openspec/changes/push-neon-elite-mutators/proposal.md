# Proposal: push-neon-elite-mutators

## Why
Chapter 2 already raises pressure, but most threats still read like stronger repeats of the same enemy roster.
We need elite enemy mutations that change priority, look dangerous at a glance, and reward aggressive clears.

## What Changes
- Add elite escort mutations for chapter 2 waves
- Add readable mutation variants for drone, charger, and sentry threats
- Add extra reward and score pressure for elite kills
- Add HUD and audiovisual callouts so elite threats are obvious in the arena

## Success Criteria
- Chapter 2 regularly introduces elite enemies without overwhelming readability
- The player can tell elite threats apart before contact
- Elite kills feel more valuable than normal kills
- Each mutation creates a different combat decision
