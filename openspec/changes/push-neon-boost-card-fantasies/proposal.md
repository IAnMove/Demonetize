# Proposal: push-neon-boost-card-fantasies

## Why
Boosts are becoming the core run identity, but the current presentation does not fully sell them as collectible cards. Passive boosts also disappear into the run summary, while action cards are the only cards that remain visibly actionable. New visual boosts should ship one at a time with clear card identity, readable effects, and spectacle on the board.

## What Changes
- Show the selected boost as a full card in the draft UI alongside its explanation.
- Add a left-side passive card rail that is visually distinct from the usable action-card hand.
- Stack duplicate passive cards on top of each other so repeated picks read as upgraded stacks.
- Give usable action cards a dramatic board effect, including a short time stop or heavy time dilation while the effect resolves.
- Add ten new visual boost concepts one by one, each with its own card, effect implementation, preview copy, and board readability pass.

## Success Criteria
- Picking a boost feels like choosing a card, not just a text option.
- Passive and usable cards are readable as different systems at a glance.
- Duplicate passive picks are visible without consuming excessive HUD space.
- Launching an action card creates a clear, high-impact moment on the board.
- Each new visual boost is independently testable and can ship without blocking the others.
