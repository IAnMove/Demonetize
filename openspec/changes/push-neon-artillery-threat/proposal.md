# Proposal: push-neon-artillery-threat

## Why
Chapter 2 already has more pressure, but most threats still collapse into direct contact, rushes, or line fire. We need one enemy that forces relocation and makes the arena floor matter more.

## What Changes
- Add an `artillery` enemy archetype that locks a strike on the arena floor.
- Add timed artillery warning circles and impact damage.
- Integrate artillery into mid and late wave compositions.
- Add minimal HUD, audio, and scene feedback for artillery locks and impacts.

## Success Criteria
- The player must reposition when artillery appears.
- Artillery must be readable before impact.
- Chapter 2 waves must feel more spatial and less purely contact-driven.
