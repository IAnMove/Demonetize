# Proposal: push-neon-rescue-spawn-signal

## Why
Rescue drops now matter mechanically, but the moment they appear needs stronger presentation so players notice the opportunity under combat pressure.

## What Changes
- Emit a dedicated event when a rescue signal appears.
- Add HUD/audio feedback for rescue signal appearance.
- Add a small visual burst at the spawn position.

## Success Criteria
- Rescue opportunities are readable immediately.
- The warning does not pause or slow the combat loop.
