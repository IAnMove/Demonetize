# Proposal: push-neon-overseer-break-window

## Why
The final Overseer fight now has more pressure, but it still needs a cleaner damage rhythm.
A short exposed-core window after the phase pulse gives the player a readable punish opportunity and makes the final pattern feel intentional instead of only louder.

## What Changes
- Add a boss-exclusive exposed-core break window after the final phase pulse resolves
- Increase damage dealt to the final Overseer while the core is exposed
- Freeze the boss briefly during the break window to create a real punish phase
- Add HUD, audio, and scene feedback so the exposed state reads immediately

## Success Criteria
- The final Overseer enters a visible exposed state after the phase pulse finishes
- The player can recognize the punish window without external explanation
- Damage dealt during the exposed window feels meaningfully stronger than normal damage
