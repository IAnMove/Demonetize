# Proposal: push-neon-overseer-collapse-finale

## Why
The final Overseer fight now has a cleaner punish window, but the actual kill still resolves too abruptly.
A short collapse finale gives the run a stronger payoff and makes victory feel earned instead of immediate.

## What Changes
- Add a boss-finale collapse event when the final Overseer dies
- Extend the final victory hold slightly for that collapse beat
- Add dedicated HUD, audio, and scene feedback for the collapse

## Success Criteria
- The final boss death reads as a distinct end-of-run event
- The player gets a brief visual and audio payoff before the victory screen
- The collapse sequence remains short and does not stall the retry loop
