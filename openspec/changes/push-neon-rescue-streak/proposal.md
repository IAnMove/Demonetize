# Proposal: push-neon-rescue-streak

## Why
Rescue saves now give direct payoff, but the loop can feel even sharper if extraction quality affects tempo and the final fiction of the run.

## What Changes
- Track consecutive rescue saves as a streak.
- Let rescue saves reinforce combo tempo.
- Reflect rescue performance in end-of-run titles and summary details.

## Success Criteria
- Saving multiple rescues in a row feels like a positive side-objective, not filler.
- Final panels clearly distinguish a clean extraction run from a careless one.
