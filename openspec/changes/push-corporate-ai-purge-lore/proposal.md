# Proposal: push-corporate-ai-purge-lore

## Why
The creator-purge premise is stronger if the player is not simply resisting the purge.
The sharper satirical hook is to make the player a corrupt moderation AI hired by a large platform contractor to erase human channels under the label of authenticity enforcement.

This preserves the current arena loop while making kills, channel avatars, and title copy feel like a bitter corporate takedown system rather than generic sci-fi combat.

## What Changes
- Reframe the player as a corporate deletion AI executing a paid purge contract.
- Reframe enemies as human channels and creator spaces being marked as inauthentic content.
- Use the generated channel-avatar atlas as enemy identity decoration.
- Add deterministic parody channel names for kill feedback.
- Add a robotic "inauthentic content" verdict when channels are eliminated.
- Keep direct real-company claims out of copy; use parody platform language and clear irony.

## Success Criteria
- The intro and title make the new satirical role clear in under 10 seconds.
- Enemy kills read as channel removals, not abstract drone destruction.
- Channel avatars render without delaying the first static boot intro.
- The voice verdict is audible after user audio unlock and does not spam uncontrollably.
- Existing combat readability and enemy telegraphs remain intact.
