# Proposal: push-neon-arena-purge

## Why
Chapter 2 and the final boss are stronger than before, but the arena itself still behaves mostly like a passive backdrop.
To make late-run pressure more memorable, the arena should become an active threat with readable purge lanes.

## What Changes
- Add telegraphed arena purge lanes
- Use purge lanes as ambient pressure in chapter 2
- Intensify purge usage during Overseer phase 2
- Surface purge state in visuals, HUD, and audio

## Success Criteria
- Chapter 2 feels more dangerous than chapter 1 even before the boss lands a hit
- The boss phase 2 feels like an arena event, not just a stat escalation
- Purge hazards remain readable and fair before they deal damage
